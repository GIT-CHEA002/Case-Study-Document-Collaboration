## Activity Diagram — Consultation and Prescription
---
The Consultation and Prescription Activity Diagram describes how a clinician records information during a patient consultation. The workflow includes entering consultation notes, recording diagnoses, selecting treatments, and recording prescriptions.

For pharmacological treatments, the clinician must enter the drug name, dose unit, total doses, and unit cost. The system then performs a dosage safety check against known safe ranges. If the dosage is outside the safe range, the system displays a warning before the prescription can be completed.

This diagram provides a process-level representation of SR-F05 and SR-F06, showing how consultation and prescription information is captured and validated.

Related Requirements: SR-F05, SR-F06, SR-F04.
